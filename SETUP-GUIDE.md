# 📚 My Cookbook Library — GitHub Pages Setup Guide

Your app files are ready. Follow these steps to get your permanent free URL.
The whole process takes about 10 minutes and is free forever.

---

## What you need
- A free GitHub account (github.com — sign up if you don't have one)
- The 4 files from this download (index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, icons folder)

---

## Step 1 — Create a GitHub account (if needed)

1. Go to **github.com**
2. Click **Sign up** and follow the prompts
3. Choose the **Free** plan

---

## Step 2 — Create a new repository

1. Once logged in, click the **+** button (top right) → **New repository**
2. Repository name: `cookbooks` (or anything you like — this becomes part of your URL)
3. Set it to **Public** (required for free GitHub Pages)
4. Tick **Add a README file**
5. Click **Create repository**

---

## Step 3 — Upload your files

1. In your new repository, click **Add file** → **Upload files**
2. Drag and drop ALL of these files/folders from the zip:
   - `index.html`
   - `manifest.json`
   - `sw.js`
   - The `icons` folder (drag the whole folder)
3. Scroll down, add a commit message like `Add cookbook library app`
4. Click **Commit changes**

---

## Step 4 — Enable GitHub Pages

1. In your repository, click the **Settings** tab (top menu)
2. In the left sidebar, click **Pages**
3. Under **Source**, select **Deploy from a branch**
4. Under **Branch**, choose **main** and folder **/ (root)**
5. Click **Save**

GitHub will show a message: *"Your site is being published"*

---

## Step 5 — Get your URL

After about 1–2 minutes, refresh the Settings > Pages page.
You'll see your URL in a green box:

```
https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/cookbooks/
```

That's your permanent app URL — bookmark it! ✅

---

## Step 6 — Add to your iPhone home screen

1. Open the URL in **Safari** on your iPhone (must be Safari, not Chrome)
2. Tap the **Share** button (box with arrow, bottom of screen)
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Name it "Cookbooks" and tap **Add**

The app now appears on your home screen with a green icon — it works like a native app, even offline! 📱

---

## Updating the app in future

If you want to update the app (e.g. Claude builds you a new version):

1. Go to your GitHub repository
2. Click on `index.html`
3. Click the **pencil icon** (Edit)
4. Replace the contents with the new version
5. Click **Commit changes**

Your live app updates within a minute or two.

---

## Your data

All your books are saved in your phone/browser's **local storage** — they stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. To back up your library, use the **⬆⬇** button in the app to export a JSON file, then re-import it on any device.
